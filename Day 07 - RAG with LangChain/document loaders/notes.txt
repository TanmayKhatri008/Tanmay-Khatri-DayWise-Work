India is one of the largest and most diverse countries in the world. Located in South Asia, it is known for its rich cultural heritage, long history, and rapidly developing economy. India is the seventh-largest country by land area and one of the most populated nations in the world. It is surrounded by the Himalayas in the north and the Indian Ocean in the south. The country shares its borders with Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar. Its capital city is New Delhi, while Mumbai is known as the financial capital of the country.

India is often called the land of unity in diversity because people of different religions, languages, traditions, and cultures live together. The country recognizes many official languages, with Hindi and English being widely used. People follow religions such as Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Buddhism, and Jainism. Numerous festivals including Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas, and Navratri are celebrated with enthusiasm across the country.
